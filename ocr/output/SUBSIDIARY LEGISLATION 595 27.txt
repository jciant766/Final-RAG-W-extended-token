Advisory Board. 
Renumbered:
L.N. 137 of 2019.
10.
(1)
The Minister responsible for the Agency may 
establish an Advisory Board to advise the Registrar of Companies in 
the discharge of his duties. 
(2)
The Advisory Board established under this article shall be 
chaired by the Registrar of Companies and shall consist of not less than 
two (2) and not more than four (4) members appointed by the Minister 
responsible for the Agency.
(3)
The remuneration paid to members of the Agency 
Advisory Board shall be in accordance with any directives and 
guidelines issued by the Permanent Secretary in relation thereto.

The Agency shall, as from the coming into force of 
this order, assume responsibility for all assets, liabilities and 
obligations, previously held or entered into by the MFSA and

Exemption from

Added by:
L.N. 137 of 2019
13.
The Agency, the members of the Advisory Board, and 
the officers and employees of the Agency, shall not be liable in 
damages or otherwise for anything done or omitted to be done in the 
discharge or purported discharge of any function under this order, the 
Act or any other legislation administered by the Agency, or otherwise 
in the exercise of their official duties, unless the act or omission is 
shown to have been done or omitted to be done, as the case may be, in 
bad faith.

Added by:
L.N. 137 of 2019
14.
The Agency shall be exempt from any liability for the 
payment of income tax, duty on transfers and documents, and customs 
and excise duty, under any law for the time being in force.
