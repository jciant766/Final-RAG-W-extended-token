Citation.
1.
The title of this Order is the Applicability of the
Companies Act to Offshore Companies formed and registered
under the Commercial Partnerships Ordinance and the Malta
Financial Services Authority Act Order.
Applicability to 
offshore 
companies.
S.L. 386.06
2.
The provisions of the Companies Act shall, as from the 1st
January, 2004, commence to apply to offshore companies formed
and registered under the Commercial Partnerships Ordinance and
the Malta Financial Services Centre Act as retained in force by
article 107(4) of the Special Funds (Regulation) Act, 2002,
(transitory provision in Part V of the last mentioned Act amending
the Malta Financial Services Authority Act, Cap. 330), and subject
to the provisions of the Companies Act (Applicability to Offshore
Companies) Regulations.
*These regulations have been repealed by Legal Notice 69 of 2024.
